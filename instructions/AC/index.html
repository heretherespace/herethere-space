Room Guide

Air Conditioner — How to Use

Daikin remote control for the room's air conditioning unit.

Remote model: Daikin ARC476A56
■
⏻
▲▼
Front face of the remote
The remote has a flip-down cover at the bottom. The three buttons above the cover (Auto/Fan, Power, Temperature) are all you need for a normal stay — everything else (mode, timer, airflow swing) is underneath the cover if you want it.
1

Turn it on / off

運転／停止 — the round button in the centre

Press once to start the air conditioner. Press it again any time to turn it off. Point the remote at the unit on the ceiling when you press it — you should hear a soft beep and see the display on the unit light up.

2

Set the temperature

温度 — the ▲ / ▼ buttons on the right

Press ▲ to make the room warmer, ▼ to make it cooler. Each press moves the target temperature by 1°C. We suggest starting around 26°C in summer.

3

Auto mode / fan speed

自動運転／風量 — the green button on the left

Leave the unit on this setting and it will automatically choose whether to cool, heat, dry the air, or just circulate it, and pick a sensible fan speed. This is the easiest setting for a short stay — you shouldn't need to touch anything else.

4

More options (optional)

Under the flip-down cover

Lifting the small cover at the base of the remote reveals further controls — manually choosing a mode (cool / heat / dry / fan only), the airflow direction, a powerful or quiet setting, and on/off timers. You're welcome to explore these, but Auto mode above covers most needs.

Not responding? Make sure you're pointing the remote directly at the unit and are within a few metres. If the display on the remote itself is blank, it likely needs two AAA batteries — please let us know and we'll sort it out.
