Stay + Gallery

HERE Senbonkita is a place to notice things — the architecture, the neighbourhood, the quiet. A renovated bunka jūtaku run as both a guesthouse and a rotating gallery, built for travel that slows down rather than rushes through.

The House

New beginnings
1970-2026

Occupying a renovated 1970s cultural-housing building in Nishi-Tengachaya, Nishinari Ward — a quiet corner of "deep Osaka," away from the tourist trail. Three guestrooms and a shared free-space, built to let guests soak in the Showa-era charm of the neighbourhood.

Location

Nishi-Tengachaya, deep Osaka

Three stations sit within easy walking distance, but the real way in is on foot — through three shōtengai that have anchored this neighbourhood since the 1970s. Many of their shutters are down for good now. We hope your stay helps keep the ones that remain open, and gives the new shops just arriving a reason to stay too.
